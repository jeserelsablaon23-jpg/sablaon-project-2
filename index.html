ABOUT KCC

Growing through knowledge, faith, and service.

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a Catholic educational institution located on Guanzon Street, Barangay 1, Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. Founded in 1927, KCC has a long-standing role in the educational life of the community.

Guided by its Catholic identity and the call to “Teach Ye All Nations,” the college seeks to nurture learners who pursue academic growth, live with integrity, and use their abilities in service of others.
